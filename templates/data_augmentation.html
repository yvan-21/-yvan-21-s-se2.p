📚 Augmentation Guide

💡
Why Augment?
Data augmentation helps prevent overfitting and improves model generalization by creating variations of your existing images.
📊
Best Practices
• Start with 3-5 augmentations per image
• Avoid extreme transformations
• Validate augmented images visually
• Balance class distribution
🎯
Recommended Settings
• Rotation: ±15°
• Zoom: 10-20%
• Brightness: ±20%
• Horizontal Flip: Yes
⚠️
Caution
Avoid augmentations that change the underlying disease characteristics. For example, extreme color changes might make not healthy images look healthy.

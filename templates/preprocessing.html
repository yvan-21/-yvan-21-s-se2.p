🔧 What the training pipeline does

  1. Load an image and apply the same green HSV mask used by train_model.py.
  2. Resize the result to 224 × 224 pixels.
  3. Convert pixel values from 0–255 to float values in the 0–1 range.
  4. During training only, generate random rotation, shifts, horizontal flips, zoom, and brightness variants.
  5. Pass the images through the CNN for species and plant-health classification.

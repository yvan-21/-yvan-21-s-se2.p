About

🌸 Orchid Identifier

An AI-powered application that helps you identify orchid species and assess their health condition through image analysis.

✨ Features

  • Real-time camera capture
  • Image upload from gallery
  • Species identification (5 common orchids)
  • Health assessment (Healthy/Not Healthy)
  • Color index analysis
  • Personalized care recommendations

🔬 How It Works

The AI analyzes the color patterns and texture of orchid leaves to determine:

  • Species based on color characteristics
  • Health status through green/red ratio
  • Color index as a health indicator
